Meet Geovanna

A trusted partner for your next move in Atlanta.

Geovanna Herrera brings a calm, detail-driven approach to one of life's biggest decisions. Based in Atlanta, fluent in English and Spanish, she guides clients through buying or selling with clarity, honesty, and genuine care.

Whether you're purchasing your first home, right-sizing, or listing a property you've loved for years, Geovanna handles every detail — pricing, negotiation, and paperwork — so the process feels considered rather than rushed. Her goal is simple: the right outcome, and a client who feels looked after from start to finish.
